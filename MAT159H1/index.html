MAT159H1: integrals, series, convergence.

The second half of the analysis sequence: integration in depth, Taylor's theorem, infinite series, and the capstone idea of uniform convergence and power series.

About this course

Where analysis gets deep.

MAT159H1 is "Analysis 1B": integrals, the fundamental theorem, elementary transcendental functions, integration techniques, Taylor's theorem, series, uniform convergence, and power series. It continues from MAT158H1. This guide uses Spivak's Calculus as a rigorous companion. Uniform convergence is the key new idea and the bridge to later real analysis.
