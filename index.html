Where This Sits

The highlighted branch is the offer.

A Mathematical & Physical Sciences offer is an offer to an admission category inside the Faculty of Arts & Science — not to a program. Computer Science and Rotman Commerce are sibling categories in the same faculty, each with its own OUAC code. Engineering and Music are the genuinely separate faculties. The program itself gets chosen at the end of first year.

Only the faculty publishes a headcount. Per-department and per-program numbers are not public, so those rows carry none — the honest size signal is the Entry column below: open means 4.0 credits and you are in, limited means you compete on grades.
