U of T · Calculus with Proofs

MAT137, unit by unit, in full.

MAT137 has no textbook — it is taught through Alfonso Gracia-Saz's video playlists and problem sets. This guide expands all 14 units, each its own page with definitions, theorems, worked examples, pitfalls, and practice.

Why this course for summer

The proof on-ramp, free and complete.

MAT137 (now offered as MAT148 + MAT149) is built for the high-school → proof jump. The same 14 topics underlie the harder analysis stream, MAT158 + MAT159, so finishing MAT137 over the summer turns September's analysis course into review-then-extend. Watch the unit video, read the chapter page, then prove the problems by hand. MAT137 video library ↗

02

How to Study

MAT137 is a flipped course: watch first, then do.

01

Watch, then prove

Watch the unit's videos, then work the problems by hand before checking. Understanding lives in the doing.

02

Do the real problem sets

The linked CC-licensed banks and past tests are the actual course problems. Use them after each unit page.

03

Master limits early

The ε–δ definition (Unit 2) is the wall. Everything downstream leans on it.

04

Re-prove from memory

Weekly, re-derive key theorems — MVT, FTC, the chain rule — with the page closed.
