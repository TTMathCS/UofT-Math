02

Probability/Statistics PhD Prep

Official specialist spine

For Mathematics & Its Applications: Probability/Statistics, treat these as the baseline course families before adding research depth.

  • MAT237Y1 or MAT257Y1
  • MAT223H1/MAT240H1 and MAT224H1/MAT247H1
  • MAT244H1 or MAT267H1
  • STA237H1/STA257H1, then STA238H1/STA261H1
  • STA347H1, MAT377H1, or MAT370H1

Top PhD theory track

For UofT, Princeton, and other highly selective U.S. PhD programs, bias toward the proof-heavy versions and the hardest analysis/probability sequence you can do well in.

  • MAT257Y1, MAT327H1, MAT337H1, MAT357H1
  • MAT457H1 and MAT458H1 if analysis is your strength
  • MAT377H1, STA347H1/STA357H1, STA447H1
  • STA422H1, STA452H1, STA453H1
  • MAT436H1 for functional analysis/operator background

Research and letters track

Prestigious PhD admissions are not just a checklist. Use upper-year courses to create research evidence and strong letters.

  • STA490Y1 or STA492H1 for communication and seminar work
  • MAT497Y1, MAT499Y1, STA496H1/STA497H1 for readings
  • STA410H1 and STA414H1 for computational statistics/ML depth
  • STA437H1, STA442H1, STA457H1, STA475H1 for applied depth
  • Prioritize A-range grades in theory courses over collecting too many electives.

Planning note. The official degree audit comes from Degree Explorer and the UofT Calendar; this PhD sequence is strategic advice for students aiming at research programs, including UofT, Princeton, Stanford, Berkeley, Chicago, Harvard, MIT, and similar departments.
